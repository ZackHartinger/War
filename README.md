<h2>How to download and play War!</h2>
<h3>Download</h3>
<p>The most straight forward way to play this game locally is to:</p>
<ul>
  <li>On the main page of the repository, click the green code button in the top right corner of the screen</li>
  <ul>
    <li>Select the Local tab</li>
    <li>Click Download Zip</li>
  </ul>
  <li>Once the file has completed downloading, extract it by right clicking and selecting "Extract All"</li>
  <li>In the extracted folder, double click the file named index.html to launch in your browser</li>
</ul>

<h3>Play</h3>
<p>The rules of War are this:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Players are each dealt 26 cards</li>
  <li>Players draw cards and compare their values</li>
  <li>The card with the higher value wins the round</li>
  <li>The player who won the round takes all cards from the play area</li>
  <li>In the case of a draw, a tie breaker ensues</li>
  <li>In a tie breaker players put 3 cards from the top of their hand face down in the play area</li>
  <li>The 4th cards are flipped face up and values are compared</li>
  <li>Winner takes all cards</li>
  <li>If there is another tie, players begin another tie breaker</li>
  <li>The first player to have all 52 cards wins!</li>
</ul>

> [!NOTE]
> I remembered after building this game that games of War can take a long time to end. This is a feature not a bug!
